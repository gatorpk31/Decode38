Privacy Policy

Axiom 38 LLC d/b/a Decode38 · Effective: May 1, 2025 · Last updated: May 1, 2026

The short version: Decode38 does not store your health information, claim responses, or personal data on any server. Your session lives on your device. You control it. You can clear it at any time.

1. What We Collect

Decode38 does not require an account, registration, or login. We do not collect your name, email, Social Security number, VA file number, or any personally identifiable information to use the tool.

The tool processes your answers locally on your device to generate estimated VA disability ratings. Those answers are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Device-Local Storage

Decode38 uses your device's local storage to save your in-progress session between uses. This data is stored only on your device — not on Decode38 servers, not in any cloud system — and includes:

  • decode38_session — your selected conditions, answers, and current progress step
  • decode38_premium — a flag (true/false) indicating whether you have completed the in-app purchase to unlock full access

You can clear this data at any time using the "Start Over" or "Clear Session" option within the app, or by clearing your browser's local storage or app data in your device settings.

3. Sensitive Health Information

Decode38 asks questions related to physical and mental health conditions in connection with VA disability claims — including questions about PTSD, military sexual trauma (MST), traumatic brain injury, and other service-connected conditions.

All of this information is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to Decode38 servers. Decode38 is not a HIPAA covered entity and does not create, receive, maintain, or transmit protected health information (PHI) in the legal sense — because no data ever leaves your device.

4. Analytics

The Decode38 website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymized usage data, such as pages visited, general location (country/region), device type, and session duration. This helps us understand how the tool is being used and where to focus improvements.

  • GA4 does not receive your condition answers or assessment responses.
  • GA4 data is collected under Google's privacy policy. You can learn more at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • To opt out of Google Analytics across all sites, use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. iOS users can limit ad tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Donations (Stripe)

If you choose to make a voluntary donation, the transaction is processed by Stripe Checkout. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch Decode38's servers. We receive only a confirmation record containing the donation amount and, if you provide them at checkout, your name and email address — used solely for our records and never for marketing.

Stripe's handling of payment data is governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

6. Feedback & Reviews

If you choose to submit feedback or a review through the site, you may optionally provide your name and email address. This information is:

  • Stored on Netlify's infrastructure (Netlify Blobs) for review and moderation
  • Never sold or shared with third parties

7. Third-Party Services

Decode38 uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymized usage analytics (see Section 4)
  • Google Fonts — typography (font files loaded from Google servers)
  • Stripe — donation payment processing (see Section 5)

We do not use advertising networks, sell data to data brokers, or share your information with claims services, law firms, or any third party with a financial interest in your VA claim.

8. Not Medical or Legal Advice

Decode38 is an educational estimation tool. It is not a medical service, legal service, or official VA determination. Your use of this tool does not establish any professional-client relationship with Axiom 38 LLC or its members.

9. Children

Decode38 is not directed at users under 17 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through this service, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Your Rights

Because Decode38 does not store your assessment data on our servers, there is no personal health data for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. For feedback data you've submitted, you may request deletion by contacting us.

California residents: Decode38 does not sell personal information as defined under the CCPA. We do not share your data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
